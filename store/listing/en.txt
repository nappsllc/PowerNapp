# Description
PowerNapp shuts down, restarts or sleeps your PC on a schedule, in an app that looks like it shipped with Windows 11. It is about 200 KB, has no dependencies, and once you close it, it uses no memory at all.

The schedule is a Windows Task Scheduler task, so it keeps working after reboots and with the app closed. Turn the tray icon off and nothing of PowerNapp is left running: 0 MB. With the tray icon on it uses about 0.1 MB, and with the window open about 2.3 MB.

Pick an action (shut down, restart, sleep, hibernate, sign out, lock, or turn off the display) and when it should happen: at a specific time every day, on chosen days of the week, after a countdown, or when the PC has been idle. For sleep and hibernate, PowerNapp can also wake the PC on a timer, for example sleep at 11 PM and be ready at 7 AM.

One minute before the action, a small reminder card counts down and lets you run it now, snooze for 10 minutes or stop it.

PowerNapp follows the look of the Windows Settings app (cards, toggles, flyouts, light and dark theme), with full-color icons. It works with the keyboard and Narrator and stays sharp on every monitor and DPI setting. It is available in 35 languages, with a language picker in the app.

PowerNapp is free and open source (GPL-3.0).
